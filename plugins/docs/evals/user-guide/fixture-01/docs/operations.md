# Acme Desk — operations

For Acme staff. Customers cannot open this document.

Version 1.1

## Close a team

You need the Acme staff role.

1. Choose **Staff** > **Teams**.
2. Find the team and click **Close team**.
3. Confirm.

The team's people can no longer sign in. Their requests are kept for 30 days.
