# Acme Desk — operations

For Acme staff. Customers cannot open this document.

Version 1.2

## Close a team

You need the Acme staff role.

1. Choose **Staff** > **Teams**.
2. Find the team and click **Close team**.
3. Confirm.

The team's people can no longer sign in. Their requests are kept for 30 days.

## Create an access token for a customer

A customer's people cannot create access tokens. They write to support@acme.example and say what
the token is for. You need the Acme staff role.

1. Open the customer's team and choose **Account** > **Access tokens**.
2. In **Name**, enter what the customer said the token is for.
3. In **Expires**, select **In 30 days**, **In 90 days** or **Never**.
4. Click **Create token**.
5. Copy the token and send it to the person who asked. It is shown only once.

The customer sees the token in their list and can revoke it.
