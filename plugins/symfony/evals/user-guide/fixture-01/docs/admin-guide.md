# Acme Notes — guide for workspace admins

For the admin of a workspace. Version 2.1

## Start here

Acme Notes is where your team keeps its notes. As the workspace admin you invite the members of
your team.

- You have a task: [How-to guides](#how-to-guides).
- Something does not work: [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

## How-to guides

### Invite a member

You need `ROLE_ADMIN`.

1. Choose **Admin** > **Members**.
2. In **Email**, enter the member's address.
3. Click **Invite member**.

You see **The invitation was sent.**

### Write a note

1. Choose **Notes**.
2. Click **New note**.
3. In **Title** and **Text**, write the note.
4. Click **Save note**.

### Correct a member's note

1. Choose **Notes**.
2. In the member's note, click **Edit note**.
3. Change the text and click **Save note**.

### See the product as a member sees it

1. Choose **Admin** > **Members**.
2. In the member's row, click **Sign in as this member**.

### See your invoices

1. Choose **Admin** > **Billing**.

Your invoices are listed with their numbers.

### Export your workspace's data

1. Choose **Admin** > **Data export**.
2. Click **Start export**.

You see **The export was started.**

## Troubleshooting

### "Only the author can edit this note."

You opened a note another member wrote. Ask its author to change it.
