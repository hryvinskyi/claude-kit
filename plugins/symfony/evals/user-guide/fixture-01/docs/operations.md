# Acme Notes — operations

For Acme staff. Customers cannot open this document.

Version 2.1

## Export a workspace's data

1. Sign in as Acme staff and choose **Admin** > **Data export**.
2. Click **Start export**.

## See a workspace's invoices

Choose **Admin** > **Billing**.

## See the product as a member sees it

1. Choose **Admin** > **Members**.
2. In the member's row, click **Sign in as this member**.
